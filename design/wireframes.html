Club Day UI: wireframes v1

v2: club logo on the key ring, a smaller screen with a soft light-blue frame, and screens that end cleanly: instead of cutting a section in half, the first screen ends with some space and a "Scroll down for…" label. Player pages only; admin has none.

Mind Club booth, Grow Pet – Grow Mind. A proposal to check before building, 30 Oct. All names and numbers are sample data. The dashed pink line on iPhone screens marks what's visible before scrolling in Safari. When a player screen has more below, the first screen ends with a little space and a black "Scroll down for…" label just above that line, and the next section starts below it. Nothing is cut in half or covered. In the app the space adjusts to each phone's height, and tapping the label jumps to the section.

  1. The phone is the Tamagotchi. Pink shell, screen, and 3 buttons (the 3 games). The egg cracks a little with each game you enter and hatches when you press Yes.
  2. The top 3 have their own colours everywhere: 1st yellow, 2nd pink, 3rd blue.
  3. The iPad shows the whole top 10 without scrolling, plus a big QR code that pulls people in.
  4. After Finish, everyone sees the same 3 prize slots on their phone, the iPad and the admin page.
  5. Spec wording stays word for word; emoji become drawn icons so they look the same on every phone.

Player · iPhone

What a visitor sees after scanning the QR code, from joining to the prize call. Drawn at 390 px wide (iPhone 12–16). Nothing is wider than the screen, so it also fits a 375 px iPhone SE; I'll test that on the real pages.

Player · iPad

If someone plays on an iPad or laptop, the device stays phone-sized in the middle. Pop-ups appear in the centre instead of sliding up.

Leaderboard · iPad at the booth

Landscape, 1180 × 760 (iPad Air / 10th gen with Safari's bar). Read from 1–2 metres away, so nothing important is smaller than 24 px. Also needs to fit a 1024 × 700 iPad, which I'll check when building.

Admin · organiser

Plain on purpose: fast to read under pressure, big buttons, no decoration. Same fonts and prize colours as the rest.
